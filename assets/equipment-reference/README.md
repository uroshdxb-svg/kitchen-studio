# Equipment reference collection 01

These unmodified product photographs are reference material for the noindex
equipment comparison page, not CC0 assets. Copyright remains with Robot-Coupe,
RONDO and Sinmag respectively. Each photograph is attributed and linked to its
manufacturer page in the comparison. Original URLs and access dates are recorded
in `equipment-model-references.json` at the repository root.

The associated geometry in `view3d-equipment.js` is authored in this repository
from those visual references, not downloaded manufacturer CAD. The eight
Robot-Coupe entries are explicitly mapped to model numbers, including separate
R 4 two-speed and variable-speed controls and Blixer scraper assemblies. The
R 301 Ultra is shown with its vegetable preparation attachment, not its detached
cutter bowl. The two sheeters are shown in working position.

Dimensions, catalogue records and verification status are not altered by this
visual pass. Geometry fits within the existing catalogue envelope. Differences
requiring variant/year confirmation:

- RONDO STM5303: catalogue 1575 × 1041 × 597 mm; manufacturer September 2024
  mechanical sheeter brochure lists 1550 × 1040 × 500 mm in working position.
- Sinmag SM-520F: catalogue 2500 × 950 × 1180 mm and 520 mm in the description;
  manufacturer's China page lists 2500 × 940 × 1180 mm and 500 mm working width.
- R 301 Ultra: catalogue 351 × 304 × 570 mm; current export page lists
  355 × 305 × 570 mm for the vegetable preparation configuration.
