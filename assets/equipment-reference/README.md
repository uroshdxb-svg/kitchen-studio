# Equipment reference collection 01

These unmodified product photographs are reference material for the noindex
equipment comparison page, not CC0 assets. Copyright remains with Robot-Coupe,
RONDO and Sinmag respectively. Each photograph is attributed and linked to its
manufacturer page in the comparison. Original URLs and access dates are recorded
in `equipment-model-references.json` at the repository root.

The associated geometry in `view3d-equipment.js` is authored in this repository
from those visual references, not downloaded manufacturer CAD. The eight
Robot-Coupe entries are explicitly mapped to model numbers, including separate
R 4 two-speed and variable-speed controls and Blixer scraper assemblies. The
R 301 Ultra is shown with its vegetable preparation attachment, not its detached
cutter bowl. The two sheeters are shown in working position.

Dimensions, catalogue records and verification status are not altered by this
visual pass. Geometry fits within the existing catalogue envelope. Differences
requiring variant/year confirmation:

- RONDO STM5303: catalogue 1575 × 1041 × 597 mm; manufacturer September 2024
  mechanical sheeter brochure lists 1550 × 1040 × 500 mm in working position.
- Sinmag SM-520F: catalogue 2500 × 950 × 1180 mm and 520 mm in the description;
  manufacturer's China page lists 2500 × 940 × 1180 mm and 500 mm working width.
- R 301 Ultra: catalogue 351 × 304 × 570 mm; current export page lists
  355 × 305 × 570 mm for the vegetable preparation configuration.

Review references:

- https://www.rondo-online.com/sites/default/files/2024-09/WEB_52641_RONDO_MAM_EN_09.2024.pdf
- https://sinmag.com.cn/en/prodetails/156/973.html
- https://www.robot-coupe.com/export/en/p/food-processors-r-301-ultra/18332

Do not generalise these models to other machines with merely similar names.
Unknown brands and models continue through the category fallback renderer.

## Collection expansion — 2026-10-29

17 further visual models use the manufacturer URLs and source images recorded in
`equipment-model-references.json`. References are for visual comparison; photographs
remain the property of their respective manufacturers. Local copies are resized
WebP files for the comparison page, not textures embedded in equipment geometry.

- Hobart HSM20: current bench mixer and wire guard. HSM10 is deliberately not mapped
  to the manufacturer's different HSM10N listing.
- Sammic BP-10/20/30/40: current keypad, guard, bowl sizes and standard column;
  optional attachment drives and I-column configurations are not represented.
- Sammic GSM-300: 300 mm blade, guard, sharpener, carriage and adjustment controls.
- Vitamix Vita-Prep 3 and The Quiet One: standard containers; Quiet One enclosure closed.
- Mahlkönig EK43 and E65S: standard black finish and full-size hopper. Regional
  dimensions/accessories can differ; catalogue envelopes remain authoritative.
- Robot Coupe J80: pulp container, feed tube, spout and drip tray fitted.
- La Marzocco Linea PB AV: separate two- and three-group source photographs and
  geometry, with the high legs shown in the references.
- Henkelman Boxer 35/42/52 and Jumbo 30: rendered closed, although photos show open
  lids. The Boxer 52 has two side seal bars; smaller models have a front bar.
  Boxer 42's current manufacturer dimensions differ from the catalogue; the
  existing catalogue dimensions have not been overwritten.

The generated `/equipment/coverage.json` inventories every catalogue row. An
individual visual model is not manufacturer CAD, a fit-out specification, or a
claim of customer approval. New catalogue entries automatically remain in the
family-model/placeholder backlog until specifically modelled and referenced.
