# Board: value, differentiation and experience

Write a short evidence brief: customer/job, current workaround, supported product, offer/price state, constraints, actual traction and unresolved assumptions. Use the current brief; leave unknown budgets and market size unknown.

Apply three passes to the same brief:

1. **Value:** Is the task painful, the result believable and the effort justified? What proof or lower-effort handoff is missing?
2. **Differentiation/distribution:** Compare real alternatives. What narrow operator moment is served better? How will public-source prospects reach the product, and what could a competitor copy?
3. **Experience/focus:** Trace the first useful concept. What can be cut? Where do equipment selection, saving, export and supplier review fail?

For each, list strengths, top three disconfirming risks, missing evidence and the smallest test. Keep disagreement. An optional score is editorial judgment, never a probability or investment endorsement. Do not impersonate or claim training/approval by famous founders.

Return `proceed_to_test`, `revise` or `pause`, with the exact condition that would change the recommendation. Give one owner and one next action; do not let simulated panel enthusiasm overrule reliability failures or missing real demand.
