# Plan: evidence before a verdict

Compile only the current relevant findings: strategy conditions, actual competition, real customer signals, offer/price state, sourced costs, product readiness and the next real experiment. Keep simulation in a separate hypothesis section; omit irrelevant framework output.

Use `scripts/decision_tools.py` for current economics and ledger evidence. Its statuses mean:

- `incomplete`: required financial inputs are absent; give the smallest missing set.
- `projection_fails`: a supplied assumption set fails contribution, operating result or capacity.
- `projection_passes_under_assumptions`: the modeled economics pass; no real-profit or demand claim follows.

Report qualified prospects, completed pilots and paying customers from evidence-backed ledger outcomes. `not_measured` is distinct from a measured zero. Never use synthetic buyer enthusiasm, a 25% panel threshold, social views or a candidate count to declare the business viable.

Write a short decision: what is known, what could kill the idea, one next experiment, owner, effort cap and acceptance rule. If the user asks for a larger business plan, reuse these source findings and disclose gaps; do not fabricate a polished missing section.

Give Product/CX/Equipment the exact blocker that matters to the next test. Keep pipeline/reply data private. Save a single consolidated packet and report completed actions separately from proposals.
