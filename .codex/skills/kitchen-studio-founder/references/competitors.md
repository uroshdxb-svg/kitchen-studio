# Competitors and substitutes

Research the exact operator job: produce a useful small commercial-kitchen concept with real equipment before supplier review. Include direct layout tools, indirect CAD/consultant workflows, supplier/dealer planning and substitutes such as spreadsheets, paper or existing templates.

Open current official product and pricing pages. Record URL, checked date, market/currency, buyer, comparable unit, inclusions/limits, positioning and verification state. Distinguish free product, subscription, one-off professional service and quotation-only pricing. Never invent a comparable price for an unavailable page.

Use reviews only for clearly attributed complaint themes; retain source, date and sample count. Check both negative and positive evidence. A handful of comments is a hypothesis, not prevalence. Respect quote limits and do not reuse competitor comments as testimonials.

Separate sourced facts from your positioning inference. Return a compact comparison, the best-supported gap, counterevidence and copying risk. If no defensible gap exists, say so. Pass exact specification discrepancies to Equipment rather than using marketing dimensions as technical evidence.

Save the research privately unless the user requests publication. Do not contact competitors, suppliers or reviewers.
