# Pricing: choose a test

Read the current validation brief and actual price/payment evidence. Preserve the first free learning pilot offer separately from any later paid package hypothesis. Do not turn an old proposed price into today's accepted price.

Compare prices from official current sources using the same unit, currency, inclusions and scope. Map the real delivery cost with the CFO module, including founder time, support, processing, hosting/AI and equipment-data maintenance allocation.

Use real buyer surveys as stated preferences only. If using four price-sensitivity questions, retain respondent/source provenance, ordered nonnegative values and sample size; do not blend synthetic answers into the sample. A stated acceptable range is not proven conversion or an automatically optimal price. When no real data exist, leave the range unmeasured.

Prepare at most two feasible price/package tests with exact contents, target, exposure/response denominators, delivery-cost assumptions, duration and a decision rule. Obtain the current task's authorization before making a paid offer or spending. Do not start payment collection before the actual commercial/payment setup is ready.

Record quoted offer, willingness statement and actual payment as distinct events. Keep guarantees, discounts and scarcity out unless they are true, deliverable and authorized. Give Growth one test proposal; give Marketing wording with price status explicit.
