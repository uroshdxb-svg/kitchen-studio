# Marketing: proof and objections

Read the current Marketing Operating Playbook, current claim/release register, approved media folder and evidence from competitors, outreach and CX. Treat consumer rehearsal as wording hypotheses only; do not select a target cohort by synthetic buy rate.

Keep one operator cohort, one useful concept, one campaign ID, one destination and one primary CTA. Write three positioning options grounded in observed workarounds and supported product behavior. Recommend one and name what evidence would disconfirm it.

Map each proposed hook to an observed/hypothetical objection and a precise proof shot. Retain the established four-core-asset campaign rather than creating another calendar. Existing approved original assets come first; an asset labeled ready needs current QA. Do not reuse the rejected reel or its unidentified duplicates.

Give Growth the channel hypothesis, accessible public audience evidence, effort/cost cap and measurable next action. Separate views, relevant replies, activation, free pilot completion and payment. A forecast contribution can inform a proposed cap but never authorize ad spend.

Return one campaign packet and Content briefs. Do not publish, schedule or send from a strategy run; honor current authorization when release is independently requested.
