# Operations: make the pilot deliverable

Map the digital service cycle: public research → source-backed qualification → exact reviewed outreach → real plan inputs → usable session → verified save/export → voluntary supplier review → feedback closure. Give each step an owner, input, output, failure condition and required availability.

Use the existing ledger, support intake, source catalogue, content tools and current native connectors. Do not build a second CRM, buy a platform or assume a mailbox/session is connected. Keep runs on demand; profile edits do not resume a stopped schedule.

Measure founder/support time, equipment-source review, per-run AI/hosting usage, error rework and handoff effort. Pass actual or explicitly hypothetical costs to CFO, keeping missing costs null. Do not treat an installed skill as proof of zero operating cost.

Equipment owns manufacturer/variant evidence and unknown utilities; Product owns truthful save/share/MCP behavior; CX owns the session and support closure. Supplier lead times and price statements require public evidence or an actual quote, not guessed values. No supplier contact is authorized by research alone.

Return a short pilot run sheet, operating risk register and cost updates. Research licenses/payment requirements only when the specific commercial setup is in scope, using current official sources. Do not borrow the upstream café-staffing/permit plan for this digital product.
