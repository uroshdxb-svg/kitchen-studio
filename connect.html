Design your kitchen by talking to your AI

Connect Kitchen Studio to Claude and describe what you need. It searches the equipment catalogue, places units in your saved kitchens, checks clearances and hoods, and you open the result in the studio to see the plan, elevations and 3D.

What it can do

read

Your saved kitchens

Lists the kitchens on your account and reads the room, walls, pillars, doors and every item.

edit

Place equipment

Adds catalogue models and fabricated stainless (tables, sinks, lowboys, saladettes, hoods), moves, resizes and removes them.

check

Check the layout

Flags overlaps, units outside the room, blocked door swings, cooking not under a hood, and returns the equipment schedule with loads.

It only touches kitchens you have saved while signed in. Drawing sets and PDF export stay in the studio. You approve access once, and you can remove the connector at any time from your AI's settings.

Connect

You need a Kitchen Studio account first: open the studio and sign in from the ••• menu, then save at least one kitchen.

Available now

Works in Claude on the web, the desktop app and mobile once added. Needs a plan that allows custom connectors; on Team and Enterprise plans an owner may need to add it for the organization.

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors (or Customize → Connectors) and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Kitchen Studio and paste the server address:
    https://kitchenstudio.design/mcp
  3. Open Advanced settings and paste the OAuth Client ID. Leave the client secret empty.
    2fa9565e-5916-4f67-9fbb-6120b7dbe11f
  4. Click Add, then Connect. Sign in to Kitchen Studio if asked and press Allow on the access page.
  5. In a new chat, make sure Kitchen Studio is switched on in the tools menu, and ask for a change to one of your kitchens.
On request

ChatGPT gives each custom connector its own sign-in address, which we have to approve on our side before it can connect. Until that's self-serve, we set it up with you.

  1. In ChatGPT, turn on Developer mode under Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced (needs a plan that allows custom connectors).
  2. Create a connector with the server address https://kitchenstudio.design/mcp and OAuth authentication.
  3. Use Report a gap in the studio's ••• menu to send us the callback address ChatGPT shows. We reply with your client ID, usually within a day.
On request

Codex can use the same tools from the terminal. It signs in through a local callback that we register for you.

  1. Use Report a gap in the studio's ••• menu and ask for Codex access. We send back a client ID.
  2. Add the server and sign in:
    codex mcp add kitchen-studio --url https://kitchenstudio.design/mcp
    codex mcp login kitchen-studio

Things to ask

List my kitchens and tell me what's in K-14.
In K-14, put two 4-drawer lowboys under the grill and burner, and keep the cooking surfaces level.
Add a double sink and a hand-wash basin on the west wall, then check the layout for clearance problems.
Find a 6-tray Rational combi oven and the best stand for it, and place it under the hood.
Give me the equipment schedule for K-14 with total electrical load.
Create a new kitchen, 8 by 5 metres, 3 metres high, with a door on the south wall.

After a change, reload the kitchen in the studio (My kitchens) to see it in plan, elevation and 3D, and to export drawings.

Kitchen Studio helps you plan; it does not replace an architect, an engineer or MEP drawings. AI changes are a first draft for a human to check. Equipment dimensions come from manufacturers' published specification sheets and must be confirmed with your supplier before ordering.
