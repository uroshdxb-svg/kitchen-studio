Five things it does that a sketch can't

Every unit on the plan is a real model with published dimensions, power, water and drain. That is what turns a picture into something a contractor can build.

01 · Real equipment

400+ verified models

Combi ovens, fryers, fridges, dishwashers, ice machines and more from the brands you'd actually buy. Tap one and the spec card opens. A missing model gets added from its spec sheet.

02 · Stainless you can spec

Tables, sinks, hoods, shelves

Fabricated modules sized in 50 mm steps with the options a fabricator needs: upstands, undershelves, pre-rinse, heat lamps. They go straight into the schedule.

03 · Plan, elevation, 3D, walkthrough

See the mistake before the slab

The fridge door that hits the pass, the hood that doesn't cover the line, the dishwasher with no landing space. Walk through the kitchen at eye height before it exists.

04 · A drawing set

What you hand the contractor

To-scale PDF: floor plan, four elevations, equipment schedule with electrical loads, and a utilities plan marking every power, water and drain point.
